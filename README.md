## The AI Research Stack

| Tool | Best For |
|---|---|
| Gemini Deep Research | Autonomous multi-hour research |
| Perplexity Pro | Real-time web research with citations |
| NotebookLM | Research over your own documents |
| Claude (with PDFs) | Analyzing specific papers/reports |

## Core Workflows

### Literature Review
1. **Landscape phase** (Gemini Deep Research) — Find key papers and themes
2. **Deep dive** (Claude with PDFs) — Compare methodology and findings
3. **Synthesis** (ChatGPT) — Write the literature review

### Competitive Intelligence
1. Research competitors with Perplexity
2. Analyze products and positioning with Claude
3. Generate competitive matrix with ChatGPT

### Investment Due Diligence
Use AI to research: business model, market size, risks,
financial performance, management, and comparable valuations.

## Research Output Templates

- Executive Research Brief (1 page)
- Competitive Analysis Matrix
- Literature Review Synthesis
- Investment Thesis Memo

## Fact-Checking AI Research

Always verify: statistics, dates, quotes, company data.
Never trust AI for recent events (check knowledge cutoff).
Cross-reference multiple sources for important claims.
